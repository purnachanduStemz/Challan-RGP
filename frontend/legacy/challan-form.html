Movement Details

Note: Tax fields apply only where the movement constitutes a taxable supply to the consignee (e.g., goods sent on approval basis). When "This is a taxable supply" is ticked, Total Taxable Value and Tax Rate are mandatory (only Cess is optional). For non-supply movements (job work, repair, testing, exhibition, self-branch transfer), leave it unticked; this challan must still accompany the goods per Rule 55, CGST Rules 2017.

Transport Details

Select No when a vehicle number is unavailable.
Required according to the applicable interstate, state, district and job-work rules.
E-Way Bill Details: Mandatory for inter-state movement above ₹50,000 and for goods sent to a job worker in another state, irrespective of value. For intra-state movement, it is mandatory above ₹50,000 in Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Kerala, Telangana and West Bengal, and above ₹1,00,000 in Delhi, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh. For Madhya Pradesh, it is not required for movement within the same district.

Tax Details

CGST and SGST/UTGST are split equally for intra-state transactions. IGST applies to inter-state transactions.

Goods

At least 1 complete item line is required. Select a product name from the suggestions or type a custom product name. HSN and GST rate are editable for each item and are required before submission. Where the rate depends on the product specification or value (for example, lanyards and badges), select the applicable rate instead of relying on the suggestion.

Return Tracking

For inputs/capital goods sent for job work, goods must be returned within 1 year (inputs) / 3 years (capital goods) of the original challan date per Section 143, CGST Act. Failing which, the movement is deemed a taxable supply with tax and interest payable.
